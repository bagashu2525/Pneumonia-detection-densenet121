How It Works

1. Upload a chest X-ray (JPG, JPEG, or PNG). 2. Click Analyze X-ray. 3. Our DenseNet121 model (via inference.py) processes the image. 4. View prediction and confidence score instantly.

About PneumoScan

PneumoScan uses transfer learning with DenseNet121 (CheXNet-style architecture) to classify chest X-rays as Normal or Pneumonia. Built for research and educational use in medical AI.
